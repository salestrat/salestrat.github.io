Privacy Policy

This page describes the information handled by the SaleStrat website as it is currently implemented.

Contacting SaleStrat. The website does not provide an account or a first-party contact form. Its contact links open your email application, WhatsApp, or Calendly. If you choose one of those services, the information you submit is handled by that provider under its own terms and privacy practices.

Theme preference. If you change the light or dark theme, the site stores that preference in your browser’s local storage so it can be remembered on your next visit. The site does not use this preference to identify you.

Website assistant. The on-page assistant uses a scripted set of responses in your browser. The current website code does not send chat messages to a SaleStrat server. If you choose an email, WhatsApp, or booking link from the assistant, the selected external service receives the information you submit there.

Third-party resources. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and the homepage loads GSAP animation scripts from jsDelivr. It also links to external contact services. Those providers may receive technical connection information when your browser requests their services. Please refer to their own privacy notices for details.

Updates and questions. Website features may change, and this notice should be reviewed again if new forms, analytics, or integrations are added. For privacy questions, contact info.salestrat@gmail.com.
