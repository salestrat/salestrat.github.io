1. Understand Your Business

Before any design work or ad spend, I look at what you're selling, who you're selling to, and what's actually stopping more of them from buying — no website, a confusing one, or an online store nobody's finding.

2. Build Or Fix What's Needed

Starting fresh, I design and build your website or store from scratch. Already online, I fix what's holding it back — listings, SEO, tracking, and ad accounts set up properly. Either way, you see progress weekly, not just at the end.

3. Launch & Keep Improving

Once you're live, I track what's actually working — traffic, inquiries, sales — and keep adjusting from there. No disappearing after handover; you get a partner who checks in and improves things over time.
