Good questions, clear answers

Before we get started

Do you work with stores outside India?

I’m based in India and can discuss projects in other markets. Fit depends on your audience, platforms, and working hours.

Can you help if I already have a Shopify store?

Yes. I can review the store, identify the priority—such as product pages, conversion, SEO, or campaign alignment—and agree on a focused scope.

Do you guarantee sales, rankings, or ad returns?

No. Outcomes depend on your offer, competition, budget, customer experience, and other factors. I’ll agree on the work and measures with you, but won’t promise a result I can’t control.

How is the work scoped and priced?

Project work is scoped around a defined deliverable. Ongoing support is scoped around priorities and cadence. Share what you need and I’ll confirm whether it is a fit before proposing next steps.

How do I start?

Send a short note about your store, the challenge you’re facing, and what you want to improve. Email and WhatsApp are both fine.

Share your store link, the problem you’re seeing, and what progress would look like. I’ll reply to discuss a practical next step.
