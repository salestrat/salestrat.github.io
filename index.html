Good questions, clear answers

Before we work together

Can you help with Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, or Flipkart?

Yes. Depending on your goals, I can help with store improvements, product and listing presentation, SEO, and digital campaigns. We’ll confirm the platform and specific deliverables before work begins.

Can you improve my Indian D2C store’s mobile shopping experience?

Yes. We can review mobile browsing, product pages, and the customer journey, then prioritize practical changes based on your store and business goals.

Can you review UPI, COD, shipping, or GST information on my store?

I can review how your existing payment and delivery options are presented to shoppers. Payment-gateway, shipping, or GST configuration and compliance work must be explicitly scoped and supported by your providers.

What should I share before we discuss a project?

Share your store or listing link, the platform you use, what you sell, and the main challenge you want to address. That gives us a practical starting point for discussing scope.

How is a project scoped and priced?

Project work is scoped around defined deliverables. Ongoing support is scoped around priorities and cadence. I’ll confirm whether the work is a fit before proposing next steps.

Do you guarantee sales, rankings, or advertising returns?

No. Results depend on your offer, competition, budget, customer experience, and other factors. We can agree on the work and relevant measures, but I won’t promise outcomes I can’t control.

How do I get started?

Send a short note about your business and what you want to improve. You can reach SaleStrat by email or WhatsApp, or book a conversation from the contact section below.
